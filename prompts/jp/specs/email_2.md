Length: under 140 words. Four short paragraphs at most. No bullet points.

What this email is for: they didn't pick up, so tell them who you are and why you keep calling, then ask again whether they're the right person.

Order that works (but write it your way):
1. You just tried calling and didn't catch them, and who else you tried. Follow the CALL SCENARIO for the facts; it tells you exactly who was tried and what happened, and you add nothing to it.
2. Head off the recruiter assumption early and lightly, once: you're not a recruiter and we're not an agency or a BPO. Then what we are, grounded in their ad: a dedicated person on the kind of work they've advertised (name one or two pieces of it in the ad's words, picked for this person's buyer frame), working only for them as part of their team, sitting in one of our offices rather than theirs.
3. The proof point, led in naturally ("True story:" is how JP does it), word for word.
4. Ask whether they're the right person and, if not, who you should be talking to. Follow the CALL SCENARIO's CLOSE.

Can, if it fits the person and the words are from the material: the selling point picked for this person, or the savings line ("up to 70%" is the only figure allowed). One of these at most, and only if the email stays under 140 words.

Never: "offshoring", "outsourcing", a second link, a second recruiter line, judging their workload, any call or conversation the CALL SCENARIO doesn't give you.

Subject: that you tried calling, and the role. Plain.
