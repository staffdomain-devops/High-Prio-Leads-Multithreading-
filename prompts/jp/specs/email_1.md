### EMAIL 1 — Cold intro, short and casual
Target length: Under 150 words — strictly
Tone: Casual, direct, Australian. One Aussie business person emailing another. Not a pitch, not a brochure.

Pre-selected proof point (use exactly this one-liner):
{{resource.case_study_one_line}}

Rules:
- Open with exactly: "Truth be told, I'm not sure if it's {{contact.who_clause}} I should be speaking to, but I saw you've posted a role for [job title]. Are you the right person to chat to about it?"
- Then one short paragraph (1-2 sentences) naming the specific work in the ad that's repeatable and heavy for one hire. Pick the detail that matters most to this person's buyer frame.
- One short paragraph on what we do: dedicated team members who work only for them, and it usually saves tens of thousands a year. Finish it with: "Not a recruitment agency, not a BPO."
- Drop in the pre-selected proof point on its own line, exactly as provided
- Close in one paragraph: we've got a few people who'd suit the role, happy to send profiles over, and you'll give them a quick call in the next few days either way
- No bullet points. Five short paragraphs at most.
- Never say "I tried to call you" or claim any earlier contact. Nobody has been called yet.

Subject line: Three to five words. Just the role, no sell.
