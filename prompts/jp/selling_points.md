# Staff Domain selling points (model-facing copy)

Edited copy of JP's selling-points document (30 Oct 2026) for the research agent. Internal-only notes, competitor names and every claim marked "verify before using" have been removed, so anything in this file may be used in an email. Pricing figures (section 15) are for the rep, never for a cold email.

Replacement guarantee wording (JP, 30 Oct 2026; matches ruling CSR-R47): if anyone leaves or doesn't work out in their first three months, we replace them free, resignations included. The original document said six months; that is superseded.

Source material for building outbound and nurture emails. Each section is a set of building blocks: short lines, proof points and objection answers that can be dropped into an email.

Built from 665 closed-won deals (Oct 2024 to Oct 2026): HubSpot calls, rep emails, Chorus transcripts, staffdomain.com and a competitor review.

**House savings line: "on average around 70%" less than hiring locally.** (JP, 30 Oct 2026; supersedes the earlier "up to 70%".) Use this figure everywhere. Do not quote 50%, 75% or role-by-role percentages in campaigns.

## 1. Core positioning

**Who we are**
- Staff Domain is an Australian-led offshore staffing company. We build dedicated teams in the Philippines and South Africa for Australian and New Zealand businesses.
- Founded in 2018 in Manila with four people. Now 700+ staff across five delivery centres: Manila, Cebu, Clark, Alabang and Randburg (South Africa).
- Staff work only for your business. They are not shared, pooled or rotated across clients.

**The one-liner**
- Skilled offshore professionals who work only for you, for on average around 70% less than hiring locally, with zero risk until you hire.

**The two classics (still use them, just don't lead with them alone)**
- Hire in weeks, not months.
- Save on average around 70% compared with hiring locally.

## 2. Risk reversal (our strongest closer)

This is the most-used argument on won calls and in won emails. Lead with it when the reader is cautious or new to offshoring.

**Short lines**
- No upfront fee. Nothing is billed until your new team member starts.
- Signing our agreement commits you to nothing. It only sets the terms if you decide to hire.
- Interview as many candidates as you like, at no cost.
- If anyone leaves or doesn't work out in their first three months, we replace them free. That includes resignations.
- No lock-in contracts. Month to month, with 60 days' notice either way.
- Our success fee is only charged if your hire makes it past month 3.

**Email-ready paragraph**
> There's no risk in getting started. You pay nothing until someone starts, and if they leave or aren't the right fit in their first three months, we replace them at no cost. No lock-in, no upfront fees, just 60 days' notice if you ever want to stop.

## 3. Transparent pricing

**Short lines**
- We put zero margin on salaries. Our flat monthly fee is the only margin we make.
- Every invoice comes with your team member's payslip, so you see exactly what they earn and exactly what we earn.
- One flat fee, whatever the role. The fee drops as your team grows.
- No hidden add-ons. Salary, benefits, equipment, HR, IT and a secure office are all included.
- In eight years, we have never applied our CPI clause.

**Email-ready paragraph**
> Most offshore providers bury their margin inside the salary. We don't. You see the payslip on every invoice, and our flat fee is the only thing we make.

---

## 4. We are the legal employer, you stay in charge

**Short lines**
- You choose and direct your people. We handle the employment, payroll, tax, leave and compliance.
- You contract with our Australian entity. Our local entity employs the staff, so you carry no employment liability offshore.
- No leave liabilities on your books. We track leave, logins and overtime and reconcile them on your monthly invoice.
- Philippine and South African labour law, contracts, 13th-month pay and government contributions are all handled.
- We're not a middleman. We're the employer, so you can manage the person directly without the legal exposure.

**Story to reference (Mel's)**
- A Philippine worker recently sued an Australian firm directly under Fair Work. Hiring through a proper employer of record removes that exposure.

**Email-ready paragraph**
> You get a dedicated team member who works only for you, without becoming an overseas employer. We carry the employment, payroll and compliance. You keep full control of the work.

## 5. Security a CFO will sign off on

**Short lines**
- ISO 27001 certified.
- Each client gets its own separated network.
- Staff work on our devices, and we can wipe a device and lock someone out at any moment.
- USB and external drives are blocked. No personal devices on the production floor.
- 24/7 guards, CCTV and biometric access. Logs are kept for 90 days.
- Police, credit, education and employment background checks before every hire.
- Accounting firms, law firms and other regulated businesses already trust us with client data.

**Email-ready paragraph**
> Data security is usually the first question, and it's the one we're proudest of. We're ISO 27001 certified, every client has its own network, and staff work on locked-down devices we can wipe instantly.

## 6. People who stay

Retention is the real cost of offshoring. Use this for buyers who were burned by staff turnover elsewhere.

**Short lines**
- Our Grit Filter tests for accountability, resilience and communication. About 30% of technically qualified candidates don't pass it.
- Voluntary staff turnover fell 46% in one year (2024 to 2025).
- Staff must give 60 days' notice, so you're never left without warning.
- Health cover of about PHP 250k per claim, versus PHP 90k to 150k at most competitors. Dependants are covered too.
- Life insurance, LinkedIn Learning, a leadership programme and mental health support for every staff member.
- Great Place to Work certified four years running (score up from 87% to 91%).
- Stevie Awards Gold and Silver 2026, HR Asia Best Companies to Work For 2026, and HR Excellence Awards Silver 2025.
- Merit pay increases are tied to your performance ratings, so you decide when a raise happens.

**Email-ready paragraph**
> Cheap offshore staff who leave in six months cost more than they save. We pay above market, cover families' health, and screen for people who stay. That's why our voluntary turnover fell 46% last year.

## 7. Managed support after the hire

**Short lines**
- A named Client Success Manager from day one.
- Weekly check-ins for the first six weeks, then monthly, plus formal 3 and 6 month reviews.
- Written KPIs from day one, so performance is clear on both sides.
- A structured four-week onboarding: orientation, security setup, systems, guided practice and a first performance check.
- 24/7 IT and HR support.
- Equipment included: dual monitors, headset and a high-spec PC, set up before day one.

**Email-ready paragraph**
> You don't have to work out how to manage someone remotely on your own. Your Client Success Manager checks in weekly for the first six weeks, sets KPIs with you, and handles the HR side so you can focus on the work.

## 8. Recruitment quality

**Short lines**
- We screen 300 to 500 applicants per role and send you the best three or four.
- Skills tests, English and cognitive assessments happen before you see anyone.
- First CVs typically land within 3 to 5 working days of your briefing call.
- Average time to hire for specialised roles: under 24 days (2024 to 2025).
- Specialist recruiters by discipline: finance, construction and engineering, IT, marketing and admin.
- Minimum three years' experience, and Australian standards experience where the role needs it.
- Before we send a proposal, we map the market and tell you how many suitable candidates exist (for example, 1,000+ for one mining role).

## 9. Flexibility

- Office, hybrid or work from home. Working from home lowers the monthly fee by about A$300.
- Your hours, your time zone. Manila is the same time zone as Perth and 2 to 3 hours behind Sydney.
- Choose whether staff follow Australian or Philippine public holidays.
- Start with one seat and grow. The fee per seat drops at 6, 11 and 20+ seats.
- Recruit in the Philippines and South Africa in parallel at no extra cost.
- Already have offshore staff? We can move them across with no transfer fee, and their tenure stays intact.

## 11. Angles competitors don't own

Use these to stand out. Never name a competitor.

- **Published replacement guarantee.** None of the big AU players publishes one. Ours covers the first three months, resignations included.
- **Zero margin, payslip shown.** Most competitors hide pricing behind a "get a quote" form.
- **Built for Australia and New Zealand only.** Several big competitors are now chasing the US and Colombia. No competitor talks about New Zealand.
- **Philippines for your day, South Africa for your evening.** Nobody else pitches South Africa to AU and NZ businesses.
- **Switching made easy.** About a quarter of our sales calls are with businesses burned by another provider. Lift-and-shift with no transfer fee.
- **Honest advice.** We tell you when not to offshore. We recommend offshoring to fill roles you can't fill locally, not to replace your existing team.
- **Ethical employment.** Above-market pay, a real manager, a real career path. Useful for supplier ESG and modern slavery checks.
